# Own bank: pre-approval + Verwertung list

Do this first — it makes every other channel work, and auctions need it (10 % security on the day, the rest
within weeks).

Ask your Sparkasse / Volksbank adviser for:

1. **Finanzierungsbestätigung** (in-principle approval) for a buy-to-let flat up to [€ …] purchase price with
   ~20 % own funds, 10-year fixed rate. Bring: last 3 payslips, employment contract, ID, overview of savings.
2. **Their property department**: ask to be told about flats they sell for customers and about repossessed
   flats (Verwertung) before they go online.
3. **Auction readiness**: can they issue a bank cheque / guarantee (Bundesbank-verrechnungsscheck or
   Bankbürgschaft) for the 10 % Sicherheitsleistung at short notice, and how fast can they pay the rest after a
   Zuschlag?
4. Their current 10-year rate for a buy-to-let loan — put it in the tracker; the scout compares it with the 4.5 %
   in the metric.

Log in `tracker.csv` (channel = `bank`).
