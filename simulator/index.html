Flat vs ETF simulator

Drag the knobs. The flat is bought with a loan, rented out, and sold at the end; the ETF gets the same starting cash. All results after tax.

When does it become passive income?

Cash left for you each month after rent, all costs, the full loan payment and tax. Bars above zero = the flat pays you; below = you pay in.

How the down payment changes things

Every other knob as set. Bigger down payment = more monthly cash sooner, but each euro of yours grows less. Values at your horizon.

How rent yield decides everything

Every other knob as set; only the rent changes. Gross yield = yearly cold rent ÷ price. A pricier flat with the same yield scales everything up proportionally; a pricier flat usually has a lower yield, which moves you left on this chart.

Model: annual steps. Loan is a fixed-payment annuity; after the fixed-rate period it is renewed at the new rate and paid off on the original date. Rent and running costs grow together. Flat surpluses and the reserve are invested in the same ETF; money you pay in is counted as ETF money you didn't invest. Sale after more than 10 years is tax-free for private owners.
